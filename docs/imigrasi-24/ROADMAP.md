- Service and journey mapping
- Regulatory/source mapping
- Knowledge governance
- Data classification
- Security architecture
- WhatsApp integration design
- Stakeholder and escalation model

## Phase 1 — MVP

- WhatsApp Business integration
- Official Answer Database
- Basic intent detection
- Passport FAQ
- Basic WNA information
- Indonesian/English
- Admin knowledge management
- Human handoff
- Audit trail
- Basic analytics

## Phase 2 — Intelligent Service

- Hybrid search
- RAG
- AI provider abstraction
- AI-assisted knowledge compiler
- Conversation context
- Complaint/ticketing
- Office locator
- Voice input
- Document classification
- Confidence and safety gates

## Phase 3 — Transactional Service

- Official service/API integrations where authorized
- Passport status
- Appointment/status information
- Ticket tracking

- Expanded WNA workflows

## Year 2 — Immigration Intelligence Platform

- Knowledge graph
- Advanced multilingual support
- Unanswered-question mining
- Service quality analytics

## Year 3 — Multi-channel Conversational Platform

- Web chat
- Mobile channel integration
- Voice channel
- Kiosk/channel adapters
- One core conversation and policy engine

## Year 4 — AI-assisted Operations

- Conversation summarization
- Complaint classification
- Response recommendations
- Duplicate case detection
- Escalation recommendations
- Operational reporting
- Anomaly detection

## Year 5 — Immigration Digital Service Intelligence

- National-scale service intelligence
- Trend detection
- Policy-impact analysis
- Public-service demand forecasting
- Continuous knowledge governance
- Provider/model-independent AI architecture

## Non-negotiable architecture goals

- AI provider is never a single point of failure.
- Known questions remain serviceable with AI disabled.
- Official sources are authoritative.
- AI-generated knowledge requires approval before publication.
- Business rules are versioned and deterministic where appropriate.
- Sensitive decisions remain under authorized human control.
- Every consequential response is auditable.
- No vendor lock-in at the application architecture level.
